Idle Time for Oct 26–27

How to fill your spare time between our 2 events.

Easy, flexible options you can do on your own or in small groups. Tap a card to see details.

Hanoi’s Must-See Attractions

2–4h · culture & city vibes

🏙️

Beyond the Old Quarter: Hoàn Kiếm Lake, the Temple of Literature, St. Joseph’s Cathedral, and the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum - a great mix of culture, history, and local life.

Hanoi highlights Food & drinks nearby
Night Market & Rooftop Drinks

Evening · vibrant & social

🌃

On weekends, the Old Quarter becomes car-free - perfect for strolling, street snacks, souvenirs, and finishing with a rooftop cocktail overlooking the city lights.

Water Puppet Show

~50 min · family friendly · 5–8€

🎭

Classic Hanoi experience near the lake - short, fun, and indoors. Vietnamese performance; audio guides available in several languages.

Thăng Long Water Puppet Theater
Bát Tràng Pottery Village

Half day · ceramics workshop & village stroll

🏺

A traditional village 30–40 minutes from Hanoi, famous for ceramics. Go by bus or taxi, try a hands-on pottery workshop (check prices in advance or ask us for tips), browse artisan shops, and enjoy a relaxed stroll. Bargaining is common.

Spa, Massage & Hair Salon

1–2h · relax & recharge

💆

A perfect reset between events. Hair-spa treatments are also highly recommended. Don’t forget to confirm the price beforehand!

Manicure & Pedicure / Lash Lift / Extensions

1–2h · highly recommended · 10–18€

💅

Treat yourself - hands, feet, or lashes. Perfect before the celebrations or as a little break. You’ll find good salons all over Hanoi; pick one nearby and ask the price first.

Silver Ring Workshop

2–3h · creative & unique · ~40€

⚪

Create your own silver ring with local artisans - a unique and personal keepsake from Hanoi. Please book in advance!

Workshop info (recommended)
Ninh Bình Day Trip

Full day · boats & karst views

🛶

Boat through Tam Cốc, visit temples, and enjoy the dramatic countryside. Do it as a classic day trip, or stay overnight - Ninh Bình has many affordable, peaceful stays. If you stay overnight, from there, you can also travel directly to Tuần Châu Island for the afterparty.

Hạ Long Bay Mini Cruise

Half day · if you’re in Hạ Long

⛴️

Short bay cruise or sunset boat - great if you extend your stay by the sea. You can also combine this with our afterparty: spend a night on a boat and go straight to Tuần Châu the next day, or plan it for after the celebration.

Side note: We'll be occupied with family events on the 26th and then heading to Tuần Châu Island on the 27th to prepare for the celebration. We can't wait to welcome you and celebrate together soon!

The Afterparty

Location: Victoria Villas - Tuần Châu, Hạ Long

Tuesday, October 28, 2025

Dress code: Beach Wedding Attire (Decorations are mainly white, beige, and green - feel free to wear any color you like!)

Kindly note on footwear:
The ceremony and vows will take place directly on the grass, so comfortable flats, elegant sandals, or heels of up to 3 cm are recommended. Dinner will also be held on the grass in front of the villas, so please choose your footwear accordingly. 🏖️🌿

Important Information for the Afterparty: We are delighted to cover the one-night accommodation (October 28–29), round-trip bus transport from and back to Hanoi, and all food and drinks during the afterparty. A separate form will be sent to confirm your attendance and travel preferences.
